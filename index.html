SilentBeep

Keep your speakers and headphones awake with inaudible audio pulses.

Download for Windows View on GitHub

What SilentBeep does

Many audio devices go into standby after a short period of silence. When you finally play a sound, you get an annoying delay while the device wakes up — and sometimes you even have to turn it back on. SilentBeep prevents the device from sleeping by periodically emitting ultra-low frequency pulses humans can't hear.

Features

Truly silent

Uses frequencies below human hearing range (10 Hz by default).

System tray

Runs in the background with a tiny tray icon and easy controls.

Customizable

Adjust interval, frequency, duration, audio device and night mode.

Auto-start

Optionally launches with Windows and auto-updates from GitHub Releases.

Private

No telemetry, analytics, or usage data is collected.

Free to use

Released under the permissive MIT license.

How to install

  1. Download SilentBeep-win-Setup.exe from the Releases page.
  2. Run the installer. It installs per-user into %LocalAppData%\SilentBeep\ without UAC.
  3. SilentBeep starts automatically and keeps your audio device ready.

Note: Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unsigned publisher. Click More info → Run anyway to proceed.
